# Content sources and illustration rights
Retrieved 18 November 2026. Educational paraphrases, not quotations or interviews.

Blue Mansion: Scottish cast-iron balusters, Cantonese timber lattices, English stained glass and Hokkien porcelain work; main house and side wings.[1]

Khoo Kongsi: Chinese clan association; approach passes the opera stage before the granite square, amidst terrace houses and shophouses.[2]

Pinang Peranakan Mansion: former residence and office of Chung Keng Kwee, now a museum with recreated displays; Chinese plates/cups and European furniture in different dining rooms.[3]

These statements supply the bilingual stories, evidence and questions in the prototype. No opening hours, admission prices or navigable visit routes asserted. Tourism Malaysia used because the mansion's own website could not be retrieved.

## Visual attribution
All SVGs in `app.js`: original George Town XR schematic illustrations, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Building silhouettes and object/detail studies are deliberately simplified: not surveyed plans, authenticated collection objects, archival images or evidence of historical alterations. Two comparison images show visual treatments, not documented historical dates. Tour viewpoints are conceptual, not an exact spatial route.

Stories are original English/Bahasa Melayu educational paraphrases of the sources below; device-generated speech is not a resident voice. Community interviews would require separate consent and credit.
